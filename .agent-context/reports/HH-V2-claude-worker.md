## Remaining Work = findings
1. **RISK (medium) — guard target thrash resets aim timers.** HumanAISystem.ts perceive (~l.123-137) now re-picks the strictly nearest threat every perception tick (10 Hz), and the aim accumulator (l.532-533, l.480) resets to 0 whenever `g.target` reference changes. Scratch test: MG guard (aimDelay 0.7 s) vs two followers whose distances swap every 6 steps: steady crowd = 36 shots in 10 s, swapping crowd = **0 shots**. A milling horde in front of an MG/tank/police (aimDelay 0.5-0.7) can neutralise the gun entirely. Fix: hysteresis (switch only when new candidate is nearer than current by a margin, e.g. d < 0.7*current d^2 or > 1.5 m closer) and/or keep aimTime when switching between zombie targets (reset only on leader<->zombie kind change).
2. **NIT — interceptor ignores the doc'd "last 0.6 m excluded" and bodies at the endpoints.** `findShotInterceptor` (HumanAISystem.ts:38-57) clamps entry t to 0 and has no endpoint exclusion. Scratch: follower 0.1 and 0.3 m *behind* the Alpha centre intercepts (0.5 m does not); a follower overlapping the shooter (0.2 m away, behind) intercepts every shot. Both need body overlap so rare; ARCHITECTURE HH-0 "Shielding" row claims a 0.6 m exclusion that does not exist. Fix: skip candidates with `t <= 0` (behind/at the shooter, `projection < 0`) and those whose centre projection > 1 (behind the Alpha), or correct the doc.
3. **NIT — Last Brain re-entry overwrites a longer grace and resets the timer.** HordeHealthSystem.ts:195 `graceLeft = entryGrace (0.4)` after a recovery (grace 1.5): recover -> follower dies next step -> grace shortened to 0.4 and timeLeft reset to 6. Recruit/die cycling can stretch Last Brain indefinitely (costly for the player, so low). Fix: `graceLeft = Math.max(graceLeft, entryGrace)`.
4. **NIT — `damageLeader` returns before the hit flash while shielded** (CombatSystem.ts:95 precedes :96), but HH-0 says "still flashes". Move `l.hitFlash = 0.2` above the shield return if the flash is wanted.
5. **RISK (low, design) — over-cap raw strength hides damage.** strength = clamp(followers + core, 0, max): brutes (3) / companions (2) can push raw above `alphaCore + cap`; killing them or emptying the core then lowers neither the bar nor `strengthLost`, and drinks/convoy heal "consume" with no visible effect (ShopSystem.ts:52 gate is on coreDamage, not on displayed strength). E.g. cap 10, 10 brutes = raw 36 vs max 16. Acceptable if intended; otherwise gate heals/HUD on visible strength or clamp raw per-unit.
6. **RISK (low, design) — critical/low level is relative to cap.** max = 6 + cap (10/nest, up to 100+). A player with 20 followers on a 100 cap is at ~19% => `hordeCritical` fires and HUD shows "critical" with no danger. If undesired, base the thresholds on `alphaCore + followers-at-peak` or only warn when strength is falling.
7. **NIT — first step telemetry.** createInitialState sets `strength=alphaCore, followerStrength=0`, so step 1 of the legacy layout (5 start followers) or any applySave'd state emits a `hordeStrengthChanged` cause `recruit` and adds +5 to `strengthGained`. Seed `followerStrength/activeFollowers` in createInitialState or skip the first-step delta.

## Probes with no finding
- applyAlphaDamage loops terminate: `nearestFollower` excludes exactly what `damageZombie` early-returns on (state!=='follower', dormant, hp<=0 incl. NaN), `amount = min(remaining, z.hp)` either kills (hp->0, state leaves 'follower') or zeroes `remaining`; no spin, no mis-count. Followers killed by splash are excluded as share recipients (hp<=0/dead); a follower hit by splash AND sharing is two distinct damage instances (by design). `strengthLost` is telemetry/cause only; strength itself is derived, so no double counting.
- Non-finite/negative incoming, restoreStrength with NaN, saved `alphaCoreDamage` NaN/out-of-range (sanitizeCoreDamage clamps to [0, alphaCore]), maxStrength can't be 0 (>= alphaCore), HUD view guards max>=1: clean.
- Death ordering: Last Brain expiry/finish -> killLeader inside updateHordeHealth, then updateRespawn (open world, same step) / checkVictory (legacy, same step). Respawn resets core+grace+Last Brain; `strengthBeforeReset` reports the reset gain once. Legacy defeat path works. Leader inside a hit in same step as recruit: strength is a once-per-step cache, a finishing hit may land in the step a recruit arrives (1-step window, acceptable).
- Perf: per Alpha hit <= 4+k scans of <= 220 zombies (+ per-hit `recipients` array and event, no per-step allocation); `findShotInterceptor` is O(zombies) with no allocation; computeHordeStrength O(zombies x companions<=few). Worst case overflow (core empty, huge splash) is O(killed x zombies) ~ 44k ops. Fine.
- Interception vs walls/height: a shot only fires with a clear LOS segment, so any follower on the segment is in front of walls; elevated shooters use interpolated height vs elevation+1.7; dormant/dead/non-follower never intercept; leader not in the zombie list. leaderTargetChance fully removed (no references in src/tests).
- Rammer damage, shop and convoy heal paths, saveSchema, Simulation order (updateHordeHealth after combat, before respawn): consistent.
- Not deeply reviewed: EntityRenderer stagger/panic and VFX/audio consumers (render-only; only skimmed).
